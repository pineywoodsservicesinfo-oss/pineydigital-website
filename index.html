Catfish King: one portal for every store

Signed, Phase 1 build starting

The problem

Catfish King's stores have reported daily sales through a vendor-hosted portal for more than 15 years. The company doesn't own it, and the rest of its paperwork travels as emailed spreadsheets. Many store managers are not highly technical, so anything new has to feel familiar on day one.

The approach

Same navigation, same fields in the same order, same reports. A manager who has used the old system sits down at the new one and needs no retraining. Improvements are limited to things nobody has to learn.

  • Totals and sales tax calculate live, per store
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly reports with one-click Excel and PDF
  • Store and corporate roles, with an audit log of every change
  • Hosted on infrastructure the company owns

The rollout

A four-week first phase, piloted at two stores. The rest follow once sales reporting has proven stable. Later phases for logs, cash audits, and documents are optional and added only when the team asks.
