FAQ

Website development questions

How much does a custom website cost?

A Starter website starts at $799 and includes 3-5 pages, custom design, mobile-responsive layout, basic SEO and a contact form. A Concept Site starts at $3,500 and includes 5-20 pages, custom design with no templates, SEO optimization, contact forms and analytics setup. Custom software platforms are quoted per project.

How long does it take to build a website?

Starter websites are delivered in 1-2 weeks. Concept Sites take 2-3 weeks. Custom software platforms take 6-10 weeks for core features, an admin dashboard and user accounts.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own everything we build, including the source code and documentation, your domain names and hosting accounts, and any business data.

What happens after the 90 days of support?

Every project includes training, documentation and 90 days of support. After that you can choose Self-Hosted (you manage updates yourself), Managed Care (we handle hosting, updates, security and priority support) or a Partner Retainer (monthly hours for new features and strategic reviews).

Which areas do you serve?

Piney Digital is based in Lufkin, TX and serves Lufkin, Nacogdoches, Tyler, Conroe, Cleveland and Houston, TX.
